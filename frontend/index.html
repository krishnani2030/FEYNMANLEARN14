Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Feynman Technique?

A learning method where you explain complex topics in simple terms, as if teaching someone else. This reveals gaps in your knowledge and deepens understanding.

How do sessions run?

Sessions run inside Feynman Learn's live room. The host and participants join from the dashboard a few minutes before start time to collaborate in real-time.

Is it free?

Yes! Feynman Learn is completely free. We believe knowledge sharing should be accessible to everyone.
